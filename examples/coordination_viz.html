Why nothing else was complaining

P2 watches role churn — and a node calmly holding everything produces none. P6 watches coverage gaps — and here every capability has a provider. The same one. Both look down an orthogonal axis, so a maximally concentrated fleet reads as perfectly healthy on every measurement that existed, right up until the node it all depends on goes away. That is why P10 exists.

What this does not claim

Rendezvous is a spread, not a bound. Three rings over three nodes still leave roughly an 11% chance one node wins all three — you may well see that above. Concentration becomes unlikely, not impossible, which is exactly why the detector stays: mitigate the cause, keep the ability to see the residue.
