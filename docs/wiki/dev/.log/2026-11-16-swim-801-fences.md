## [2026-11-16] ingest | #801's last round: SeqCst fences on the removal admission; the Ping arm's re-check tested

- The removal stores the removed set then clears `peers`; an inserter stores `peers` then re-checks the set. When
  the member is not yet in `peers` the clear writes nothing, so it is the store-buffering litmus: both loads can
  miss under acquire/release (and x86). `fence(SeqCst)` on both sides: `membership_ops::apply`, the TCP Ping arm
  (sender and piggybacked peers), SWIM's `BecameAlive`. No test can make a fence fail deterministically.
- The Ping arm's sender re-check gained a test-only hook (`connection::TEST_BEFORE_PEER_INSERT`) and
  `lib_tests::test_a_removal_between_the_ping_arms_check_and_insert_still_wins`.
