## [2026-11-20] ingest | I7-S, PR 1: a grant decision names what its decider saw

A grant decision now names the request record's tool, resource, arguments digest and expiry (`Shown`, signed after
the grade; a bearer's beside its body), and the addressed node compares them with its own record field by field
(`decision_names_another_request`), after checking that record against itself for every decision. A service's or a
bearer's decision naming nothing is refused by default; `gateway_accept_unnamed_decisions` admits it, counted, for
one release. Invariant worth keeping: **the node compares the digest as the record's string, never recomputed** — it
may hold no arguments, and a pre-I3 record names the legacy form. The provider is unchanged: it trusts the issuer
for the decider's half.

Still open, and the release blocker: the replay half of #813's F1 (a tombstoned `…/issued` re-admits the same body,
now only for the request it names, inside an admission span of up to 900 s of node time at the default drift) — PR 2.
