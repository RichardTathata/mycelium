Relays for an indirect probe are `Alive` members only (suspects are
targets, not relays); a removed member (C5) is shut out of SWIM — its datagrams dropped, never probed or
rejoined, a `BecameAlive` for it kept out of `peers` — and `docs/design/member-removal.md` no longer claims
the TCP connection loop covers SWIM; the rejoin probe is spawned; the targeted sample holds no more entries at
`n = 0`. Stated limit: a seedless node in no one's `bootstrap_peers` is still not rejoined. Behaviour changes:
`docs/guide/deprecations.md` §113.
