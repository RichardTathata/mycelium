## [2026-10-24] ingest | adopter-surface X3, first PR: a grant — approval that is default-deny

- The design fact the row did not know: the appointment model (`GrantVerifier::check`) allows **one holder per scope
  and epoch** (two mandates at one epoch in one scope are `ConflictingAppointments`), so per-call grants cannot share a
  resource authority's scope. Each grant has its own scope `grant:{request}`, the issuing node is entitled under the
  prefix (`entitle_grants`), the verifier keeps no epoch for grants, and `assess` skips the resource gate for a grant
  while keeping the revocation standing of its term. The binding (arguments digest, policy revision) rides **inside
  the signed operations** (`bound:args:`, `bound:policy:`), so `Mandate`, its canonical bytes and the 20 struct
  literals across the workspace are untouched.
- The agent's door is `transition_invoking(tool, arguments)`: the request record under `grant/{agent}/{request}`, the
  wait on the KV prefix watch plus the timer seam, expiry on `decision_now_ms`, verification by the node's own
  authority with a possession proof signed by the node's identity, then the commit. A plain `transition` to a
  grant-required tool refuses: it carries no arguments to bind.
- The supervisor's door: `GET /gateway/grants/pending`, `POST /gateway/grants/{agent}/{request}`; issuing runs the
  preflight with the issuer as actor (`grant.issue`), so a scope is not an approval; the node signs the grant; the
  write answers with its rung. `grant/` is route-owned.
- Pages: `docs/features/grants.md` (registry, glossary `grant`), guide 21's new section, `deprecations.md` §59, RBAC
  rows, the plan §8 X3 row with what the first PR leaves.
