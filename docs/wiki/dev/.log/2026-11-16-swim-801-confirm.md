## [2026-11-16] ingest | #801's confirm round: removal re-checked after every peers insert; peer exchange filtered

- The TCP Ping arm checked only the sender against the removed set and inserted every piggybacked `known_peers`
  entry, so a node that had not applied a removal re-introduced the removed member (every tick with SWIM off). It now
  skips removed members (`lib_tests::test_piggybacked_removed_member_not_added`).
- Every insert into `peers` (the Ping arm's sender and piggybacked peers, SWIM's `BecameAlive`) re-checks removal after
  it inserts and removes the member again: `membership_ops::apply` writes the set, then clears `peers`, so whichever
  order they interleave in, one side sees the other (`swim::tests::a_removal_between_the_check_and_the_insert_still_wins`,
  a test-only hook between check and insert).
- The `BecameAlive` gate's own test: a third party's `Alive(r)@1` gossip
  (`swim::tests::a_third_partys_gossip_does_not_readmit_a_removed_member`).
