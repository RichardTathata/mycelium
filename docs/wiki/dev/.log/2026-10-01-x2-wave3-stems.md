**Durable knowledge:**
- **A librarian manages only its own key's lines.** It publishes and tombstones catalogue entries
  signed by its publisher, so an agent's proposal lives in the catalogue beside the manifest, not in
  it — and retiring v1 is an edit to the manifest, which the librarian turns into a tombstone. That is
  why the librarian is now a stem of its own: if it doubled as a provider, killing the incumbent could
  kill the only process that retires v1.
- **The late provider is the guaranteed non-incumbent.** Two stems that both see unmet demand on the
  same round may both install; starting provider-c only in wave 3 makes "the host that serves the
  accepted v2" a host that never held v1.
