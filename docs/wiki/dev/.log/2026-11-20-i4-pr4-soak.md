- **The soak** (`src/agent/call_soak_tests.rs`): cancellable calls through a minting gateway to an enforcing
  provider, three doors (`/a2a`, `/mcp`, the gateway's dispatch with a grant in slot 1), half cancelled while
  running, every one reconciled — registrations, bound 1 from the revocation to the confirmed stop, a stop record
  per cancelled call and no other, views and records back to nothing. 10³ in CI on the `compliance,a2a` line; 10⁵
  `#[ignore]`d, run once and dated.
- **It passes over a gateway with no `[persistence]` and fails over one with it.** The collector's minute pass
  (`call_issuer::collect`) deletes every record past its floor at once; each delete is a WAL append that
  `SyncMode::Flush` syncs alone; a revoke or a mint is `kv_set_requiring_sync`, which is durable first — no store
  entry, no frame, no forward until its own sync returns. So a cancel asked during the pass is held for the pass.
  Pinned by `a_cancel_is_on_disk_inside_bound_1_while_the_collector_deletes_a_backlog` (ignored: it fails today).
  Not fixed: pacing the collector, an unsynced tombstone, or a WAL that commits a queue under one sync are each a
  decision (§9 O10). **Exit 14 stays open; the row is not closed.**
- **An execution authority never forgets a `grant:` scope** (`RevocationView::forget` is called for `call:` scopes
  only): the soak's provider ends with one `grant:` scope per both-slots call. A ledger row, not I4's to fix.
- **The figures on the shipped code** (exit 18) and **the memory per held window** at the 64-byte term cap: the
  ledger rows. Resident, a held scope's view is about 1.4 KiB, twice S5's count by structure; `MAX_CALL_WINDOWS`'
  doc comment is corrected.
- **Testing lore** — what a soak over two in-process nodes has to arrange (a registered tool runs one call at a
  time; time a bound from the event it is stated from; a failure at the same count each run can be a clock) —
  is on [testing](../testing/testing.md) § *A soak over two in-process nodes*.
