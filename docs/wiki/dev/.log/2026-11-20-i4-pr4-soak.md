## [2026-11-20] ingest | I4, PR 4: the soak, the figures, and the defect the soak found (row I4 not closed)

`docs/plans/identity-and-lifecycle.md` row I4 records PR 4 (*PR 4 (evidence)*) and §9 O10;
`docs/operations/what-is-proven.md` rows `call-soak`, `call-mandate-carried-checkpoint-cost` (the spike's row,
replaced), `call-window-memory`, `call-cancel-behind-collector`, `authority-grant-scopes-not-reclaimed`,
`audit-chain-grows-per-call`.

- **The soak** (`src/agent/call_soak_tests.rs`): cancellable calls through a minting gateway to an enforcing
  provider, three doors (`/a2a`, `/mcp`, the gateway's dispatch with a grant in slot 1), half cancelled while
  running, every one reconciled — registrations, bound 1 from the revocation to the confirmed stop, a stop record
  per cancelled call and no other, views and records back to nothing. 10³ in CI on the `compliance,a2a` line; 10⁵
  `#[ignore]`d, run once and dated.
- **It passes over a gateway with no `[persistence]` and fails over one with it under `sync_mode = "flush"`.** The
  defect, as #816's review corrected its scope: **one sync per synced append, served in arrival order.** A revoke or
  a mint is `kv_set_requiring_sync` — durable first, no store entry, no frame — and a cancel's forward waits for its
  revoke, so the provider's stop is held, not only the acknowledgement (work on the gateway node itself stops at
  once). Under `flush` any burst of appends holds a cancel (the collector's minute pass is how the soak met it);
  under any mode required-sync writes queue behind each other; under the default `async` the collector holds
  nothing. Pinned by `a_cancel_is_on_disk_inside_bound_1_while_the_collector_deletes_a_backlog` (ignored: it fails
  today; it asserts against the quiet write, not a wall clock). Four candidate fixes — pace the collector, unsynced
  collector deletes, WAL group commit, forward beside the durable write — are with the maintainer (§9 O10).
  **Exit 14 stays open; the row is not closed.**
- **Fixed, from the review: a stopped call could be answered `not_running`.** A cancelled work guard removed its
  registration (row 48) before it kept its stop record (row 49); `call_cancel::answer` reads both, in that order. The
  record is now kept first. A reader of the two must see the registration, the record, or both.
- **What the soak's process held** (833 MiB resident after 10⁵ calls) is mostly the compliance audit chain — 2.6
  `sys/audit/` records a cancellable call, at every node, until an operator prunes — then `call/` tombstones (50
  minutes) and grant use markers (7 days); not a leak in I4's structures. Resident set cannot say whether memory
  fell; live heap can. A ledger row for the audit chain's growth.
- **An execution authority never forgets a `grant:` scope** (`RevocationView::forget` is called for `call:` scopes
  only): the soak's provider ends with one `grant:` scope per both-slots call. A ledger row, not I4's to fix.
- **The figures on the shipped code** (exit 18) and **the memory per held window** at the 64-byte term cap: the
  ledger rows. Resident, a held scope's view is about 1.4 KiB, twice S5's count by structure; `MAX_CALL_WINDOWS`'
  doc comment is corrected.
- **Testing lore** — what a soak over two in-process nodes has to arrange (a registered tool runs one call at a
  time; time a bound from the event it is stated from; a failure at the same count each run can be a clock) —
  is on [testing](../testing/testing.md) § *A soak over two in-process nodes*.
