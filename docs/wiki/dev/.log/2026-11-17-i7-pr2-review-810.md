## [2026-11-17] ingest | I7 PR 2, #810's review: where a grade is read from, and how it reaches the envelope

Every finding fixed. Finding 1 — a node that never read the newer certificate read the older one's grade — by the
maintainer's decision of 2026-11-17: the grade is bound outside the KV, on the `gateway_consent_principals` entry
(`signed_with`, required), and a decision is admitted only when the body, the certificate and the entry agree. The verified `signed_with` is the evidence journal's and now names the certificate it was read from;
the copy on a refusal's or revocation's KV record is stated as unsigned. The verified decision reaches the preflight
as a typed value only `http/grants.rs` builds, not through `params`. `consent_grades_unread` and
`consent_certificates_provisional` are on `/stats`. `SignedDecision`, `Rule` and `RuleDecl` are `#[non_exhaustive]`.
A consent principal is refused as a grant holder; a consent entry's name must be one a certificate can carry;
`Rule::accepting_grades` refuses an unknown grade.
