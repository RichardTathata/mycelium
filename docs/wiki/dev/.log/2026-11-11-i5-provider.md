## [2026-11-11] ingest | I5 PR 1: the provider verifies an OIDC user's exchanged token

`dev/security.md`: a provider whose `[oidc]` sets `exchange_audience` verifies the exchanged token (`ot`) at the receive
boundary. It checks, in the row's order:
- `so`, the gateway's signature over the token;
- the JWKS signature, read from the gateway verifier's cache without waiting;
- audience and `azp`;
- `cnf.jkt` against the envelope's signer;
- the subject;
- the lifetime;
- the `jti`, once per provider.

An `oidc:` action without an `ot` is refused. `dev/concurrency/lock-order.md` gains row 78 for the window. Plan I5
(PR 1 of 2), feature page `docs/features/gateway-sso.md`, `deprecations.md` §96.
